How it all started

From our kitchen to your table.

Our baker started making cardamom-spiced cakes at home and sharing them with friends and family. Soon, those first slices led to requests for birthdays, gatherings and just-because days. What began as a few cakes for the people closest to us grew into Crumb & Cardamom: a small home bakery making thoughtful treats by hand, one batch at a time.
